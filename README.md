# 🌿 DesiCut — Student Nutrition & Fat Loss Hub

**DesiCut** is a sleek, mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA) built specifically for students and bachelors. It focuses entirely on **affordable, local whole-food nutrition** (eggs, rice, lentils, local fish, milk, and oats) without any expensive supplement or protein powder overkill.

Designed with an immersive **iOS Glassmorphism UI** (OLED dark mode, frosted glass blur effects, and a floating glass dock), DesiCut makes healthy eating and fat loss effortless on campus.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Dynamic Macro Onboarding:** First-time users enter their name, height, weight, body fat level, and goal to instantly calculate a personalized daily calorie deficit and protein target.
* **Bangladeshi Student Presets:** Pre-loaded with practical meals tailored for mess systems and university life (Oats, eggs, local fish, *musoor dal*, *muri*, and *khichuri*).
* **Whole-Food Swaps:** Easily swap standard meals for budget-friendly desi alternatives while tracking macros.
* **Quick-Add Desi Estimator:** Instantly calculate estimated calories and protein for unplanned street food or canteen snacks (*tehari*, *singara*, *paratha*).
* **Floating Glass Dock:** A futuristic floating navigation bar pinned to the bottom for effortless one-handed thumb navigation.
* **Local Persistence:** Automatically saves your custom profile, daily routine, and 7-day streak locally in your phone's browser storage.

## 📱 How to Install on Your Mobile Device

### On iPhone (iOS):
1. Open your live GitHub Pages URL in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button in the bottom toolbar.
3. Scroll down and select **Add to Home Screen**.
4. DesiCut will launch seamlessly like a native app right from your home screen!

### On Android:
1. Open your live GitHub Pages URL in **Google Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three vertical dots (menu)** in the top right.
3. Tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.
4. DesiCut will appear on your home screen and open in full-screen mode!

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** Single-file HTML5 / Vanilla JavaScript (No heavy build steps or frameworks)
* **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (via CDN) with custom iOS Glassmorphism variables
* **Hosting:** GitHub Pages
