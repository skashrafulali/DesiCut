# 🌿 DesiCut — Student Nutrition & Fat Loss Hub

**DesiCut** is a sleek, mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA) built specifically for Bangladeshi students and bachelors. It focuses entirely on **affordable, local whole-food nutrition** (eggs, rice, lentils, local fish, milk, and oats) without any expensive supplement or protein powder overkill.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Bangladeshi Student Presets:** Pre-loaded with practical meals tailored for mess systems and university life (Oats, eggs, local fish, *musoor dal*, *muri*, and *khichuri*).
* **Whole-Food Swaps:** Easily swap standard meals for budget-friendly desi alternatives while tracking macros.
* **Quick-Add Desi Estimator:** Instantly calculate estimated calories and protein for unplanned street food or canteen snacks (*tehari*, *singara*, *paratha*).
* **iOS Glassmorphism Design:** Deep OLED dark mode with frosted glass blur effects (`backdrop-filter`), smooth Cupertino animations, and custom SVG app icons.
* **Floating Glass Dock:** A futuristic floating navigation bar pinned to the bottom for effortless one-handed thumb navigation.
* **Local Persistence:** Automatically saves your daily routine and 7-day streak in your phone's browser storage.

---

## 📱 How to Install on Your iPhone

1. Host this project on **GitHub Pages**.
2. Open your live URL in **Safari** on your iPhone.
3. Tap the **Share** button in the bottom toolbar.
4. Scroll down and select **Add to Home Screen**.
5. DesiCut will now launch seamlessly like a native app right from your home screen!
